Domain overview

{{ project_title }} is a Python assistive system built with OpenCV and YOLO26 and an open-vocabulary YOLOE model for real-time object and obstacle detection, an NLP module for natural scene descriptions, gTTS for speech, and GPS + Folium for live mapping, all served from one Flask web application.

Core focus: convert what the camera sees into natural spoken language while sharing the user's live location with caregivers. Detection, description and speech happen automatically, with no manual input.

Problem statement

The gap: a cane or guide dog cannot describe surroundings. Reading text, recognising obstacles and sharing an emergency location are poorly served by low-cost tools.

The impact: limited independent mobility reduces confidence in unfamiliar places, and late obstacle awareness increases the risk of falls.

Our goal: a system that detects objects through a camera, turns them into natural spoken feedback, and shares live GPS location, all in one Flask web app.

What it can do

Obstacle guidance

YOLO26 + YOLOE detection of 140 everyday object types with left / ahead / right position and distance.

Collision warnings

Time-to-collision tracking with evasive spoken commands.

Sign translation

EasyOCR text extraction translated into 14 languages.

Medicine reader

Glare-reduced foil OCR with expiry-date warnings.

Banknote recognition

₹10 to ₹500 notes from colour profile and OCR tokens.

Crossing assistant

Traffic-signal state with safe-to-cross advice.

Find my item

Spatial audio radar that beeps faster as you aim at the item.

Campus wayfinding

Turn-by-turn walking directions between landmarks.

Fall detection

Accelerometer impact detection with a cancellable SOS countdown.

Horn & siren alerts

Microphone spectrum analysis with haptic warnings.

Tele-assistance

Caregivers see the camera view and speak guidance.

Hands-free control

Voice commands and keyboard shortcuts for every tool.

Architecture & workflow

1 · Vision

Camera frames processed with OpenCV, YOLO26 and YOLOE for objects, obstacles and spatial position.

2 · Language

NLP scene descriptions translated by NMT into 14 regional and international languages.

3 · Speech

gTTS, with browser speech as fallback, reads descriptions aloud hands-free.

4 · Location

GPS tracking and a live Folium map on the caregiver dashboard.
