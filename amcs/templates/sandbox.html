Viva defence quick reference

How does the system stay real-time on a phone or CPU?

Small YOLO26 weights (with the YOLOE vocabulary model on every third frame), background threads for OCR and speech, and selective frame skipping keep the video at full frame rate while detection takes about 0.1–0.2 s per frame on a laptop CPU. The browser draws the HUD locally, so the video never waits for the server.

How does collision avoidance (time-to-collision) work?

The tracker measures how fast each moving object's bounding box grows over time. When the estimated time-to-collision drops below 2.5 seconds, an urgent evasive instruction overrides normal speech.

How are users protected from expired medicine?

CLAHE glare reduction makes foil strips readable. OCR extracts EXP and MFG dates with regular expressions, compares them with today's date, and speaks an urgent warning if the medicine has expired.

What reassures caregivers during an emergency?

A fall (accelerometer spike above 2.5 g) or the SOS button shares live coordinates on the caregiver map and creates a WhatsApp message with a Google Maps link. A 10-second spoken countdown lets the user cancel false alarms.
